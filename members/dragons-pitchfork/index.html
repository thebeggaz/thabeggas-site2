I came in through a beat tape and some rookie rhymes. Kwan carried my tapes to Father Lord, and that got me into what was still called the Shaolin Clan. Lord and King Cee had seniority on the boards, so I fell back and watched. I got more serious about my rhymes. I honed those rhymes at a glass kitchen table in Digital Kwan's first apartment, pushing pens in front of a boombox.

At The Temple, Lord taught martial arts, philosophy and science mostly by example. In all those sessions he gave me exactly one explicit tip on a beat. Everything else I learned by watching him work. While he was still with us I only produced two songs for Tha Beggas. I made Zen Master for him, and Wen Tzu for him and Buddha Love.

As short aXe I rhymed on songs like Super Natural, Bloody Parry and Ju Va De. After Lord passed in 1997 I walked away, and short aXe stopped fitting. It took years and more than one name, YukoN BLaCK among them, to find my way back. Dragon's Pitchfork is who came out the other side.

"Mental planes fly to Venus on the White Owl's wings"short aXe on Super Natural, 1997

These days I build out loud. In 2021 I co-founded GRYSKLL, a Hip Hop & Creative Wellbeing collective, and I run House of Fluid Reflections with my wife, Yani. I reconnected with my brothers in 2024. In 2025 I performed in the Super Natural video. Now I'm filming my chapter of Killa Bee Movie, the story of The Killa Bee That Couldn't, and helping bring what Lord left in the vault into the light.
