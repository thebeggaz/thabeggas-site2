Founder · In memory, 1997

FATHER
LORD

Wu ChiThe LZAThe Drunken Scientist

Founder of Tha Beggas. eMCee, producer, mentor and kung fu practitioner. His sound shaped the group and reached across the Wu-Tang extended family.

On record

Role
Founder, eMCee, producer
From
Washington, D.C.

On the Strength landed on Wu-Tang Killa Bees: The Swarm in 1998 and again on Wu-Tang Collective in 2003. Father Lord didn't take a verse on it. DJ King Cee scratched his voice into the hook. The Swarm went Gold on February 10, 1999.

He's also on the hook of Tai Chi, from Killah Priest's Heavy Mental.

"I penetrate from the gateless gate"Father Lord, Zen Master

Most of what he left is still in the vault: demos, cassettes and DAT tapes. The work to preserve it is ongoing.

He died in a drunk driving incident in 1997. To the people who knew him he was a teacher, a martial artist and a brother.
