House Rules

Shake loose when they grab you. Duck the high stars, flip over the low ones. Small hoppers only go down low.

Weapons are earned on the climb. The sword first, then the steel fans, then the double short axes. Clear the library to earn the Dragon's Pitchfork. On the roof, the Black Dragon wakes.

Lose all three lives and you get ten seconds to continue from the floor you fell on. Your score starts over.

Each keeper fights you on a raised floor. Knock them off the edge and the duel is yours. Same goes for you.
